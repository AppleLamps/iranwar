Maritime focus

The Strait became the pressure point

Iran's effort to regulate, charge, delay, and narrate traffic through the Strait of Hormuz became central to the war. The United States answered with blockade language, interdictions, mine-clearing, escorts, and Project Freedom.

Nuclear track

The record repeatedly returns to one threshold: no Iranian nuclear weapon, with US demands centered on highly enriched uranium, enrichment suspension, and facility dismantlement.
